01 — Foundations

Design Principles

Six convictions that keep every screen on-brand. When a decision is unclear, these win the argument.

① Optimistic Futurism

Design like the future is a vacation, not a dystopia. Upward angles, rising suns, forward motion — the tomorrow promised on a 1962 postcard.

② Exotic Escapism

Every interface is a little getaway. Saturated desert-tropical color gives even a settings page a sense of somewhere-else. Delight is a feature.

③ Architectural Texture

The breeze-block screen is the brand's signature surface — used the way it is in buildings: sun-screens, dividers, façade lattice. Pattern carries identity.

④ Warm, Never Grey

Neutrals are sand and midnight indigo — never corporate grey. Heat lives in the palette. Even shadows lean warm. If it feels sterile, it's wrong.

⑤ Honest Structure

Desert modernism prizes clean planes and clear function. Generous whitespace, strong grids, legible hierarchy. Flourish sits on disciplined bones.

⑥ Tactile & Physical

Hard offset shadows, thick outlines, spring motion. Controls look pressable, like enamel signage and chrome trim. Built, not rendered.

Contrast rule (fixed in v2)

White text fails on aqua and flamingo. So: ink text on bright fills (aqua, flamingo, lime, amber, coral) and white text only on dark fills (terracotta, palm teal, ink). Body text is ink-on-sand or sand-on-midnight. Bright hues are for fills, borders, and large display — never small text on white.

Roughly 60% warm neutral, 25% primary aqua, 10% one supporting warm, 5% a single electric pop for the thing you want clicked.

07 — Application

Do & Don't

✓ DO
  • Let warm neutrals dominate; treat bright accents as precious.
  • Use breeze-block screens as low-contrast texture behind, beside, or above content.
  • Put ink text on bright fills; white only on Deep / Danger.
  • Keep one neon/script flourish per view.
  • Anchor playful color on a disciplined, generous grid.
  • Drive everything from semantic tokens so light/dark just works.
✕ DON'T
  • Don't run body text directly over a busy breeze-block lattice.
  • Don't set white text on aqua, flamingo, lime, or amber.
  • Don't set body or labels in Monoton or Pacifico.
  • Don't reach for cold corporate grey or techy blue glow.
  • Don't use blurry drop shadows where a hard offset belongs.
  • Don't hard-code hex in components — reference tokens.
